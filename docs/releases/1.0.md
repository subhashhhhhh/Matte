The first release of Matte: Instagram as a native Mac app, made for the desktop rather than a phone app stretched to fit.

**Your feed, laid out for a wide screen.** Posts in columns, stories at the side, carousels you can swipe with the trackpad, songs and reels that play when you rest on them, and double-click to like.

**Messages, live.** Chats arrive the moment they're sent, with reactions, replies, voice notes, shared posts and reels, pinned chats and messages, nicknames, and notifications.

**Reels and stories.** Full size, with a mini player that keeps a post playing in the corner while you browse.

**Post from your Mac.** Photos, carousels and reels, and a story editor with text, drawing, stickers, filters and music. Add posts and reels to your story, too.

**Everything else you'd expect.** Profiles, Explore, search with your recent searches, saved posts and collections, likes lists, tagged people, multiple accounts, light and dark mode.

**Ghost mode.** Watch stories without marking them seen, read messages without sending "Seen", and type without showing "typing…". All optional, in Settings.

**Light on your Mac.** A fraction of the memory instagram.com takes in a browser.

Matte isn't affiliated with Instagram or Meta. It signs in on Instagram's own page and keeps your session only on your Mac.
